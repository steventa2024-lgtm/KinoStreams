## Expanded collection — 45 portraits

These local WebP delivery copies retain the source composition. They were proportionally resized (maximum edge 512px for portrait/square images, 768px for most wide stills) and encoded at quality 85 for quick delivery. Circular framing and face focal positions are applied in CSS; no backgrounds or character details were generated or replaced. Images below are downloaded once and served locally, with no third-party image requests at runtime.

The Fusion-Profile-Avatars links are a **community mirror**; its platform labels are not independent verification of original platform provenance. TVmaze character records supplied the credited TV portraits. Other sources are the linked studio/franchise galleries or credited editorial publications.
